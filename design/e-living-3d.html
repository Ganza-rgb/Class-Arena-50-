Original 3D, no emoji

Trophy, crowns, coins, glass like-bubbles, bell, megaphone, warning sign, gift box and glass chart are modelled in code (design/3d/arena3d.js) with real reflections and refraction. Avatars are our own blinking mascots: no stock photos of real people.

Motion that “tricks the mind”

  • Idle float & slow sway (4–8 s cycles)
  • Objects lean toward your pointer
  • Mascots blink, breathe, follow your cursor
  • Numbers count up, bars fill, light sweeps buttons
  • Respects “reduce motion” · toggle above

Safe by design

Shout-outs are anonymous but moderated: class leaders see reports first. Points are only for fun rewards (homework pass, snacks, mascots), never money.
